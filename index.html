💀 MUNCHING 💀

Once Upon a Time...

The world has fallen into ruin. You are an adventurer who has come to challenge Lord Munching, the monster who rules over the world. You intend to enter his tower and slay Lord Munching. You must fight your way from Floor 1 all the way to Floor 10.

Tower Rules

  • ⚔️ Enemies: Defeat enemies by having ATK equal to or higher than their Power.
  • ❌ Defeat: Fall down 2 Floors.
  • 🌀 Traps: Watch out for deadly traps.
  • 💀 Death: If your level falls to 0, the game is over.
  • ⚖️ Balance: Monsters, Traps, and Items have an equal chance (33%).
  • 🏃 Run: Roll 1–2 to fall 2 floors, 3–4 to escape and lose 1 random equipment, or 5–6 to escape safely.
